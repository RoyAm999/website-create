## Working product prototype

A business enters its name, public WhatsApp mobile number, a tariff and service cities, confirms public sharing and creates a shareable link. The customer enters sofa size, material, additional mattresses, city, preferred timing, name and optional notes. An unlisted city can be written explicitly.

Standard in-area cases show a provisional tariff calculation. Special material or an unknown service area requests manual review instead of inventing a price. The customer reviews the resulting message, can copy it, or explicitly approves the displayed recipient before opening WhatsApp. Opening WhatsApp is not sending, delivery, a reservation or a sale. Automated tests only inspect fixture URLs and never open or send to the fixture recipient.

The sample has fictional prices and no recipient. Customer entries remain in browser memory and disappear on refresh. They are not saved to the clinic database. A photograph is attached manually later in WhatsApp; image upload/transfer is not implemented or claimed.

Configuration lives publicly in the URL fragment. It is unsigned, modifiable and cannot be centrally revoked. Business ownership and prices are not verified. The output is a request for review, never a trusted or binding quote. A paid production service would need verified ownership, persistent/revocable configuration and an appropriate operational/data-handling setup.

## Business interpretation

The former product prepares and prioritizes manual recovery work. It can support an operator, but no observed incremental revenue currently justifies a recovery promise. More visual polish is not demand validation.

The new hypothesis is a done-for-you branded website/intake installation for a narrow local-service segment, initially upholstery cleaners. The intended value is less clarification work before a useful quote, not automatic creation or recovery of leads. This competes with existing forms and websites. Public examples such as H Clean's photo-based quoting process and Factory Clean's structured WhatsApp form establish workflow relevance and competition, not demand for this prototype.

Suggested next gate: one suitable business with existing enquiries, its own actual tariff, a paid setup commitment and observation of form completion, clarification-message burden and quote turnaround. Keep booking outcomes separate. If the form increases abandonment or owners assign no value to the saved work, stop rather than add another dashboard or manufacture a monthly subscription.
