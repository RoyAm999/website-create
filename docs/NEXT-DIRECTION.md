# Alternative business hypothesis, not a declared pivot

Roy likes the original guest workspace but does not see a reason a clinic would pay. The current app offers prioritization and manual message preparation; it does not itself deliver outreach, guarantee bookings or prove incremental revenue. More design does not establish demand.

Instead of deleting the working application, /next/ explores an adjacent but materially different task: a customer-facing structured price-request link for upholstery cleaners. A provider configures public contact and a tariff. A customer describes the job, sees a provisional estimate when eligible, reviews a message and explicitly hands it to WhatsApp. The provider does not have to operate a separate inbox. The example can be used without an account.

## Why this hypothesis, and its limits
Research on September 5, 2026 found existing Israeli service businesses that request sofa photos and scope before pricing. That establishes a real workflow, not willingness to pay us. This isn't novel technology and competes with forms, custom websites and WhatsApp itself.
- https://www.cleanbrothers.co.il/ describes pricing by item, material, condition, quantity and location.
- https://hclean.co.il/ asks for a photo before quoting and uses direct WhatsApp contact.
- https://factoryclean.co.il/ already offers an on-site cleaning request/estimate path. Evidence of competition, not a source to copy branding or testimonials.

A proposed first offer is a done-for-you branded intake installation for one business, not a generic monthly SaaS. Do not claim a market price or sell promised conversion gains. Suggested validation: observe a small permitted sample of actual enquiries, demonstrate the custom flow, seek a paid setup commitment; then compare follow-up-message burden, completed enquiry share and quote turnaround. Track actual bookings separately. Forms can increase abandonment and some customers prefer a photo/chat; keep direct contact available in a real rollout. Do not proceed to broader build based on compliments alone.

Cancelled-appointment filling was also considered. It already exists in local and international products:
- https://simpletor.app/ describes waiting lists and notifications on cancellations.
- https://www.phorest.com/features/ includes calendar gap optimization and waiting lists.
- https://www.gettimely.com/pricing/ includes waitlists in specified plans.
We therefore do not label that an unoccupied niche.

## Operational boundaries
Prototype config is public, modifiable, unsigned and cannot be centrally revoked. Prices are estimates requiring manual verification by the provider, never trusted signed quotes. The link exposes only intended public business info, never customer input. No cloud database, API credentials or customer records copied. No authenticated account claimed to be repaired. No automatic send, image attachment transfer, payment, calendar hold or conversion analytics. The generated WhatsApp link is only opened at the customer's explicit click; automated tests never contact its fixture recipient.

This is not ready for processing sensitive clinical information. Do not put secrets in the public configuration. A production paid rollout needs verified business ownership, persistent revocable configuration, privacy review appropriate to actual data, operational support and measured demand. Existing Shuv Flow remains intact during the comparison.
