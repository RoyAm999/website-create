# Shuv, next direction
Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. A separate prototype, not an approved commercial pivot.

## Evidence and authored decisions
1. Vibe: decisive, graphic, industrial, human. User asks for a completely different design, not a polish. Authored references: thermal job slips, industrial switchboards, oversized magazine type.
2. Journey: recognize repetitive price questions; see scattered questions assemble into a structured request; try the customer-facing experience; configure an actual shareable intake link.
3. Energy: bold opening, quiet definition, one spatial transformation, usable demo, decisive chartreuse close.
4. Feeling curve: recognition (four familiar questions), skepticism (explicit prototype boundaries), relief (questions converge into one request), control (a real estimate changes from the visitor's inputs), agency (create your own link).
5. Signature: a four-question exploded brief becomes one coherent work request under native scroll. It is the site where the questions you always repeat fold into one ready-to-review request.
6. Aesthetic: graphic industrial, near-black #121615, cool ivory #F1F3EE, one acid yellow-green #D7F563. Not the previous cream/olive SaaS palette.
7. Separate scenes, not one continuous world. New grammar: Request instrument. The visitor assembles a request, not watches a product dossier. Bans: card-grid feature section, invented social proof, fullscreen video, continuous camera flight, moving primary controls.
8. Existing assets: use original master logo; do not invent a replacement. Render software/paper objects in HTML/CSS, no photographic claim or stock customer faces. No image generation or Kie spend required.

## Depth contract
Far plane: 3D wireframe rule bands and oversized letterform, low displacement.
Middle: partial request strips, independently translated and rotated.
Focal plane: a coherent structured paper form; stable fields and edge light.
Foreground: a thin acid registration rule crosses the papers, not the headline.
Typography/buttons: semantic, stable, readable in first viewport and motion-off.
Mobile: distinct composition, no pin, complete final brief. No horizontal page overflow.

## Grammar gate (against Shuv Flow Casework)
Different grammar (request instrument vs casework); nav (instrument rail vs evidence jumps); hero (oversized typographic/folded form vs inquiry dossier); sequence (question/fold/input/handoff vs file/match/process); close (owner configurator vs scenario entry); signature (request assembler vs reason switch). 6/6 planned differences; not a claim of originality across all sites.
Rejected filmic: no footage/continuous journey needed. Chaptered editorial: tool is more important than essay. Live surface default: risks another dashboard pitch. Gallery: no collection. Atlas: no browsing world. Conversation: request form is not a chatbot. Casework: the previous rejected marketing grammar. Defined new constrained grammar is permitted by the skill.
