Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. User: “תעשה כניסת אורח לבנתיים ... יש לך יד חופשית”.

## Evidence and authored decisions
1. Audience: Hebrew-speaking clinic owners recovering existing unclosed inquiries. Tone: calm, precise, human, decisive. No invented revenue promises.
2. Journey: recognise forgotten conversations → reject generic follow-ups → discover a concrete new reason → try the real matching logic → enter the working product without an account.
3. Energy: dimensional open, quiet typographic contrast, one interactive peak, decisive close.
4. Feeling curve, before act planning: recognition (a paused conversation); relief (permission not to spam); agency (a business change selects precisely the relevant people); trust (traceable steps and explicit limits); readiness (guest access).
5. Signature: The Second-Chance Switch. Change the clinic fact, watch eligible people change, inspect the factual old/new link, and take the same selected scenario into the guest workspace. Derived from real conservative matching rules, explicitly sample data.
6. Range: warm minimal product editorial, not luxury cosmetics, no anonymous stock clinic imagery. Preserve master logo and original cream/olive palette. Light, low-density working interface with mobile bottom navigation.
7. Distinct short scenes. Native scrolling, no cinematic film or artificial worldflight. Custom grammar: Casework. Navigation jumps to evidence and experiment; opening is the paused lead; middle is a usable experiment; ending opens that exact scenario in the application. Bans: video, huge empty pinning, invented proof counters, automatic message dispatch, fake login, decorative dashboards, lock/capture of pointers.
8. Assets: existing shuv-flow-logo.png, existing matching/CSV safety code. No new logo, imagery API, video, or font binaries. Scroll-craft engine pinned to 0b816225945e45380397d6a0487efa3c98916858 and unmodified. Native font stack.
